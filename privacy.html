Dodgewing Privacy Policy

Effective: November 10, 2026
Developer: GZS Studio
App: Dodgewing

This policy describes how information may be collected, used, and shared when you play Dodgewing. Please contact us if you have questions.

1. Gameplay and accounts

Dodgewing is designed to work without a player account or sign-in. The game may store gameplay settings and progress locally on your device.

2. Advertising

Dodgewing uses or is designed to use Google's AdMob advertising service for banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. When ads are enabled, Google and advertising partners may process device and app information, IP addresses, advertising identifiers (where available), ad interactions, and diagnostics. This can support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and service operation. Ads may require network connectivity even if gameplay is available offline.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures for more information.

3. Data sharing and retention

Advertising-related data may be shared with Google and applicable advertising partners. Third parties have their own privacy and retention practices. GZS Studio does not claim all ad data is retained only on your device.

4. Your choices

You can manage advertising and privacy preferences through your Android device settings and any consent prompts available in the app. Uninstalling Dodgewing removes app-local data subject to device backup settings but does not necessarily delete data already processed by third parties.

5. Children and audience

The game's audience settings and any child-directed advertising controls must be configured according to its actual intended audience and Google Play requirements.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated as the game, SDKs, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit the GZS Studio support page.
